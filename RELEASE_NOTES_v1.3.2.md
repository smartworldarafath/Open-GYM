# Open-GYM v1.3.2 (Beta 2) Release Notes

Welcome to **Open-GYM v1.3.2 (Beta 2)**! This major release delivers modern Apple-style Transparent Blur Liquid Glass UI across all key screens, seamless in-app auto-updating with 100% data retention, and full multiplatform support for **iPhone, iPad, MacBook, Windows, Linux, and Android**.

---

### ✨ What's New & Highlights

#### 1. 💧 Apple-Style Transparent Blur Liquid Glass UI Overhaul
- **Home Screen**:
  - **Start Workout Button**: Refined frosted glass pill with optical border sheen, internal caustic highlights, and responsive tactile press physics.
  - **Folders (Routines, Tools, Journal)**: Transparent liquid glass flap with real-time backdrop blur, displaying underlying workout content.
  - **Streak Pill**: Frosted glass flame indicator for daily consistency tracking.
- **Progress Screen**:
  - Top-right circular translucent liquid glass action icon.
  - Social workout export sheet featuring a full frosted glass **Share** button.
- **Exercises Screen**:
  - Top-right circular liquid glass **'+' (Create Exercise)** action pill.
- **Profile Screen**:
  - Translucent liquid glass **Edit Profile** button layered over the cover banner.
  - Dual circular liquid glass quick-action icons for sharing and settings.
- **Settings Screen**:
  - **UI Color Switcher**: Floating glass dock with dynamic accent glow.
  - **Units Switcher (kg / lb)**: Segmented frosted glass switcher dock.
  - **My Places**: Presets (Gym, Home, Outdoors) and "+ New place" with glass styling.
  - **Import Data**: Frosted glass **Choose File** launcher.
  - **About & App Updates**: Ambient glass hero card and frosted **Check Again** button.

---

#### 2. ⚡ Seamless Direct Updater with Zero Data Loss Guarantee
- **In-App Direct Installation**: Update directly from **Settings > App Updates** with one click.
- **Zero Data Loss**: Your entire workout history, custom exercises, routines, photos, notes, and profile settings are 100% preserved.
- **No Uninstall Required**: Upgrades install seamlessly over existing installations across Android, Windows, macOS, Linux, and iOS.
